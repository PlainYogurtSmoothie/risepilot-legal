# risepilot-legal

Privacy policy for RisePilot, an alarm app published on Google Play by Plain Yogurt Smoothie.

Published at https://plainyogurtsmoothie.github.io/risepilot-legal/

`fgs-demo.mp4` is the demo video for the Google Play foreground service
declaration: a Quick Alarm rings over the lock screen while its sound plays in
a `mediaPlayback` foreground service. Recorded on a Galaxy S22 without audio.
